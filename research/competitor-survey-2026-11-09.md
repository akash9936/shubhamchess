# Competitor survey — 20 chess academy sites (read 2026-11-09)

Sites: Neelesh, Sathurangam, KKR, WonderKids, Indian Chess Academy, Envision, Birati,
Darklight, Joshi, Gupta, School of Chess, Mumbai Chess Academy, ChessEasy, ChessPure, Archer,
SA Castle, GKS, Nasa, UpZen, Sathuranga Chanakyan. Homepages read by four web-fetch agents;
most subpages not read. Figures are the sites' own claims, unverified.

## What nearly every site does (15+ of 20)
- Free demo / trial / assessment as the ONE primary CTA, repeated 4–6× down the page.
- WhatsApp link with a prefilled message; phone number visible; floating WhatsApp button on
  ~half the sites.
- Level ladder (3–6 tiers, often with rating bands) so a parent can place their child.
- Coach credentials: FIDE title/rating, often a link to the FIDE profile.
- Parent testimonials on the homepage; student results with ratings/places.
- A stats strip: students taught, years coaching, countries, tournaments.

## Common but not universal (6–12 of 20)
- FAQ on the homepage · "Not sure which level?" picker · 4-step "how it works" ·
  countries/flags strip · blog/guides (SEO) · Google rating badge/reviews ·
  video (coach intro, class clips, parent video reviews) · gallery · social links.

## Rare, but differentiating
- Public pricing (4 of 20: ICA, Gupta, ChessEasy, UpZen; Sathuranga "from ₹…").
- Playable puzzle / free tools (UpZen puzzle trainer, ChessEasy tools + quiz).
- Urgency ("limited slots this month": Mumbai, WonderKids, Shubham's poster).
- Refund guarantee after first 2 classes (WonderKids). Sibling discount (Darklight).
- WhatsApp-composed demo "form" — 3 fields → wa.me message, zero backend (Gupta).
- Calendly booking + Google Form apply, zero backend (GKS).
- Public schedule grid of slots (Sathurangam, Joshi per-venue).
- Verifiable proof: FIDE profile links, dated event photos, certificates (School of Chess).
- Lead magnet PDF: none of the 20 had one.

## Needs a server (seen on sites; DECLINED for ours per CLAUDE.md)
Student/parent portal + login (Envision, SA Castle, UpZen, Archer, ChessPure, KKR,
WonderKids classroom subdomains) · enquiry forms that store/email (Darklight, Nasa, Archer,
Joshi, ICA, School of Chess) · payments/invoices · newsletter signup · AI game reports.

## Quality traps seen (don't copy)
Animated counters that read "0+" when JS fails (Neelesh, WonderKids, Envision, ChessEasy);
stock/randomuser avatars on reviews (SA Castle); inconsistent counts across the page;
lorem-ipsum testimonials (Joshi); dead "#" CTAs; placeholder phone numbers.
