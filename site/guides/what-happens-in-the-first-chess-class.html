What happens in a first online chess class?

By Shubham Singh, Arena International Master and FIDE rated coach. Updated 9 November 2026.

The first class at this academy is a free assessment. It is a real lesson, not a sales call, and it ends with a clear suggestion about level and format. Here is what to expect.

Before the class

  • Device: a laptop or tablet with a camera. A phone works but the board is small.
  • Connection: steady home Wi-Fi. Sit near the router if the signal is weak.
  • Software: classes run on Zoom or Google Meet. The link arrives on WhatsApp. Lichess or Chess.com is used for the shared board, and a free account is enough.
  • The room: a quiet corner and, for younger children, a real board next to the screen. Moving a wooden piece helps the idea land.
  • You: for children under eight, a parent nearby for the first ten minutes helps with the technology. After that, step back.

Minute by minute

  1. Hello and a question or two (5 minutes). Has your child played before, with whom, and what do they like about it. The coach is listening for vocabulary: does the child say "horse" or "knight"?
  2. The moves (5 to 10 minutes). For a beginner, the coach checks each piece's movement with a quick game. For a child who already plays, this is skipped.
  3. Three or four puzzles (10 minutes). Mate in one, then a simple fork, then something a step harder. The coach watches how the child looks at the board, not only whether the answer is right. A child who checks the whole board before moving is already ahead.
  4. A short game (10 to 15 minutes). Coach against child, with the coach talking through some moves. This shows attention span, how the child reacts to a mistake, and whether they can explain a move in words.
  5. Five minutes with you. The coach suggests a level, a format (small group or one-to-one), and two or three time slots. You can decide later on WhatsApp.

What the coach is really looking for

Not talent. Attention, curiosity, and how the child handles being wrong. Those three predict progress far better than how many openings a nine-year-old can name.

After the class

You receive a WhatsApp message with the suggested level and the class plan. If you join, the first regular class is usually within the same week.
